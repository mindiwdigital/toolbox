About your Mindiw Toolbox

App information

Mindiw Toolbox works on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile browsers. Camera access requires HTTPS (or localhost) and your permission. For best results, use an up-to-date browser.

🔐
Privacy firstPDF creation and image processing happen locally in your browser. No account is required.

Install app

No app pop-up will interrupt you. Android: open the browser menu and choose Install app. iPhone: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Your phone may show its own final system confirmation.

Storage & permissions

Camera access is requested only when you start scanning. Downloaded images may appear in your phone's Downloads or Photos app depending on device settings. Use the Share button to choose a gallery/photos destination where available.
