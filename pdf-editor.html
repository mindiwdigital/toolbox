What you can do

  • Merge — combine several PDFs, or PDF pages and photo scans, into one document.
  • Split — export every page as its own PDF and download them in a ZIP.
  • Reorder — move pages up or down until the sequence is right.
  • Rotate — turn any page in 90° steps, individually.
  • Crop — drag the page corners to trim edges or straighten a photo.
  • Page numbers — add a small "page / total" footer on export.
  • Extract text — run text recognition on your pages and copy or download the result.
  • Save pages — export a single page as an image file.

Importing an existing PDF

Choose Open PDF in PDF Studio and select one or more files. Page thumbnails are generated so you can see exactly what you are working with, and imported pages keep their original quality on export. Password-protected or damaged files cannot be opened.

Building a document from scans

You can start from the camera scanner or your photo library and add imported PDF pages into the same document. That makes it easy to place a signed cover page in front of scanned pages, or append a downloaded form to your own photos.

Exporting and sharing

Give the document a name, pick A4 or US Letter, set the image quality and create the PDF. The finished file downloads to your device, is saved to the app's local My Files library for reuse, and can be shared directly with your phone's share sheet where supported.

Frequently asked questions

Can I merge several PDFs into one?
Yes. Import multiple PDF files and their pages are added to the same document in order. You can also mix imported PDF pages with your own photo scans.
How do I split a PDF?
Use the Split pages action to save each page of the current document as a separate PDF and download them together in a ZIP file.
Can I reorder and rotate pages?
Yes. Open a page in the full-page editor and use Move up, Move down or Rotate to change its position and orientation.
Can I crop a scanned page?
Yes. The crop tool shows four draggable corners so you can select the exact page area and straighten it.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No. PDFs are loaded and rebuilt in your browser. Text extraction and editing happen on your device, and your files are not uploaded.
