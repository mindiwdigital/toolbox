How to create a QR code

  1. Open Mindiw Toolbox and go to Quick Utilities → QR creator.
  2. Choose Website address or Text or message.
  3. Type your link or message — links are checked automatically so mistakes are caught early.
  4. Tap Generate QR code, then download the PNG or copy the encoded content.

Link and text modes

In link mode the address is normalised and validated, so a missing https:// is added for you and obviously broken addresses are flagged before you print anything. Text mode accepts any message, including UTF-8 characters such as Sinhala, so you can encode more than just URLs.

High-resolution downloads

QR codes are rendered as vector-crisp images and exported at a high resolution, which keeps the squares sharp when they are printed on posters, flyers, cards or packaging instead of just viewed on screen.

Offline and private

The QR engine is bundled inside the app rather than loaded from a third-party service, so codes are generated on your device and keep working without a connection. The content you encode is never sent anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator free?
Yes. Creating and downloading QR codes in Mindiw Toolbox is free, with no account and no watermark.
Do QR codes stop working after a while?
No. These are static QR codes, so the data is stored directly in the pattern. They keep working as long as the link or text they contain is valid.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The QR engine is bundled with the app, so QR codes can be generated without an internet connection once the page has loaded.
Can I create a QR code for plain text instead of a link?
Yes. Switch to text mode and enter any message, including non-English characters, and a QR code will be generated for it.
What size is the downloaded QR image?
The QR image downloads as a high-resolution PNG suitable for printing on flyers, posters and packaging.
