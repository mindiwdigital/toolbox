Scan documents with your camera

Use your phone camera or laptop webcam to capture clean, straightened document pages. Mindiw Toolbox detects the page edges, corrects the perspective and enhances the text, then lets you export everything as a tidy PDF — without uploading anything.

What you can scan

  • Paper documents, contracts and forms
  • Receipts, invoices and bills
  • Handwritten notes and notebook pages
  • Whiteboards, business cards and ID documents

How to scan a document

  1. Open Mindiw Toolbox and go to PDF Studio → Scan camera.
  2. Allow camera permission when your browser asks.
  3. Frame the page so all four corners are visible and hold the camera steady.
  4. Tap the shutter. The page opens in the full-page editor, where you can crop, rotate, change the filter or sign it.
  5. Add more pages, then choose a paper size and export a single PDF.

Automatic edge detection and straightening

As you aim the camera, the scanner looks for the page outline and highlights the detected corners. When you capture, that quadrilateral is used to straighten the page so a slightly angled photo still comes out flat and square, ready to read or print.

Colour and black-and-white scanning

Pages are saved in colour with automatic contrast and shadow cleanup so text stays crisp. If you prefer a smaller, high-contrast file, switch the page filter to Black & white or Grayscale in the editor — you can do this per page.

The full-page editor after capture

Every page opens in a dedicated full-screen editor, so you review one page at a time instead of a cramped grid. From there you can change the filter, crop the page corners, rotate it, add a signature, reorder pages, or save and share a single page as an image.

Tips for sharper scans

  • Use even, bright lighting and avoid hard shadows across the page.
  • Keep the whole page inside the frame with a little margin around the edges.
  • Hold the camera as parallel to the page as you can.
  • For very small text, move closer rather than zooming digitally.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app to scan documents?
No. Mindiw Toolbox runs entirely in your web browser. Open the site, allow camera permission and start scanning. You can also add it to your home screen so it behaves like an installed app.
Are my scanned documents uploaded to a server?
No. Photos and scans are processed on your own device using the browser's camera and canvas features. Nothing is uploaded to Mindiw servers.
Does the camera scanner work on a laptop?
Yes. On desktop and laptop the scanner uses the built-in or connected webcam. On phones it prefers the rear camera for higher quality pages.
Why does the scanner ask for camera permission?
Browsers require your permission before a website can use the camera. Camera access also requires a secure HTTPS connection (or localhost).
Can I scan several pages into one PDF?
Yes. Capture pages one after another and they are collected into the same document. When you finish, choose your paper size and quality and export a single multi-page PDF.
