What you can do

Resize

Set the exact width and height in pixels to shrink a photo for the web or enlarge a thumbnail. Dimensions are capped at 12000 px per side, and the app shows the original size so you can compare before downloading.

Compress

Export as JPG and use the quality slider to reduce file size. This is the quickest way to make a photo small enough to email or upload while keeping it easy to view.

Convert

Switch formats between JPG, PNG and WebP. Use PNG when you need lossless quality, JPG for photographs, and WebP for the smallest files on modern websites.

How to use Image Tools

  1. Open Mindiw Toolbox and go to Image Tools.
  2. Choose an image from your device.
  3. Enter a new width and height, pick a format and set the quality.
  4. Tap Download processed image to save the result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image to exact pixels?
Open Image Tools, choose an image and type the width and height you want in pixels, then download the processed image.
How do I compress an image to make the file smaller?
Choose JPG as the output format and lower the quality slider. A lower quality produces a smaller file; adjust until the size and clarity suit you.
Which image formats can I convert between?
You can convert to and from JPG, PNG and WebP, depending on the formats your browser supports. WebP is often the smallest for the web.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No. Resizing, compressing and converting all happen in your browser using canvas, so your image never leaves your device.
Is there a size limit for images?
Image dimensions are capped at 12000 pixels per side. Very large images also depend on your device's available memory.
